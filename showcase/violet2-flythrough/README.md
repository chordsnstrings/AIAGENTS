# Violet 2 — TH12-4M townhouse flythrough (three.js)

Scroll-driven, real-time 3D flythrough of **plot XJ407B** (unit `VOT2/SD143/XJ407B`), a
four-bedroom **TH12-4M** mid-terrace townhouse in **Violet 2, DAMAC Hills 2, Dubai**.
The camera behaves like a stabilised drone: an aerial approach over the community, an
orbit to the street, then through the front door and every room, a reverse pull-out over
the garden, up the stair core to the roof terrace at sunset, and a blue-hour pull-back that
leaves the lit house on screen beside the summary.

```bash
npm install
npm run build     # bundles src/ -> dist/app.js (three.js r187 + n8ao)
npm run serve     # http://localhost:8080
```

Open `index.html` through any static server (module scripts do not run from `file://`).

## What is real and what is illustrative

Real, from the developer documents (sales offer + Violet brochure p.27):
plot 6.10 × 18.50 m (112.85 m²), room positions and sizes, three levels, two covered
parking spaces, saleable areas (589.22 / 632.92 / 296.12 / 834.00 sq ft = 2,352.26 sq ft),
facade language (white render frame, grey stone, bronze railings and lattice screen,
slatted timber pergolas), community drive times and amenities.

Illustrative: furniture and finishes, landscaping, the neighbouring cluster layout
(curving back-to-back rows modelled on the sales-offer location plan), lagoon and dunes.
The page says so in its summary card. Price and payment plan are deliberately not shown.

## Structure

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `src/plan.js` | Single source of truth: plan coordinates (x across plot, d from street, z = -d), levels, rooms. Drives both the 3D model and the SVG mini-plans. |
| `src/builder.js` | Accumulates boxes/quads/geometry per material key and emits one merged mesh (or one `InstancedMesh`) per material. Box UVs are in metres so textures tile at real-world scale. |
| `src/house.js` | Exterior shell of one townhouse. Hero copy has real balusters and open doors; neighbours get alpha railing cards and fake lit interiors. |
| `src/interior.js` | Hero-only interior: floors, linings, stacked dog-leg stair, doors, kitchen, bathrooms, furniture, interior lights. |
| `src/community.js` | Curving rows of 7-unit blocks around the hero (mirrored alternates), LOD blocks with a facade atlas further out, streets, lamps, palms, lagoon, desert. |
| `src/materials.js` | PBR sets from CC0 photo-scans (Poly Haven, ambientCG) packed as WebP colour / normal / AO-roughness; shader patches for tile joints, macro variation, texture contrast, fresnel glass, palm sway and the baked-GI lookup. |
| `src/gi.js`, `src/gi-data.js` | Baked irradiance volume (generated by `scripts/bake-gi.mjs`; do not edit the data file) mixed per moment into two 3D textures. |
| `src/softgoods.js` | Procedural duvets, pillows, throws and sheers: smooth noise plus a drape rule, no runtime simulation. |
| `src/atmosphere.js` | Preetham sky with lit clouds, sun + shadow zones, PMREM environment from the sky, fog, stars, time of day. |
| `src/path.js` | Keyframed drone route: positions on a centripetal Catmull-Rom, orientation (yaw, pitch), fov and lens shift on monotone cubics; gimbal rig (lag, bank, hover, view offset). |
| `src/story.js` | The one chapter table: card windows, rail labels, where a rail click lands, the day-to-night ramp, the sun swing and the plot-pin windows. |
| `src/post.js` | N8AO ambient occlusion, volumetric light, bloom, ACES output, grade/vignette/grain, SMAA. |
| `src/volumetric.js` | Shadow-map raymarched light shafts and haze, dust motes, lamp cones. |
| `src/styles.js`, `src/stylesets.js` | Interior style presets (materials, named by the albedo a finish should read as) and their furniture sets. |
| `src/ui.js` | Loader, chapter cards, rail, mini-plans, printable plan sheet, plot pin, keyboard chapter navigation, live region. |

## Route and chapters

`src/story.js` holds the thirteen chapters with their progress windows; `src/path.js` keys the
camera to the same numbers, and `index.html` cards name their chapter with `data-chapter`.
Changing a chapter's timing is a two-file edit (table and keys); the cards, rail, time-of-day
ramp, sun swing and pin follow.

| Chapter | Progress | Shot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arrival | 0–0.04 | aerial, the house centred and pinned from the first frame |
| The community | 0.056–0.13 | descent over the lakes and terraces to the plot |
| Facade | 0.146–0.215 | orbit down to the street in front of the house |
| Ground floor | 0.235–0.31 | carport, front door, a glance right through the kitchen opening, the hall |
| Living | 0.335–0.452 | an arc along the living room looking at the sofa, table and glazing, turning back towards the room |
| Garden | 0.474–0.505 | reverse pull-out through the open panel to a hold over the rear wall: patio, lit glazing, balcony |
| Bedrooms | 0.521–0.58 | in from the rear balcony to a level hold just inside the glazing: bed, prints, lamps, the wardrobe wall by the door |
| Master suite | 0.602–0.664 | through the door to a 3/4 view from the foot of the bed, the front glazing, sheers and lattice as the key light |
| Front balcony | 0.68–0.712 | out through the open panel to a hold at the balustrade over the two loungers, lattice at the left, then a reverse dolly back through the room |
| The stair | 0.728–0.784 | into the stair well and up to the roof landing and its window |
| Bedroom 4 | 0.80–0.866 | from the stair top through the doorway past the open leaf to a hold inside: bed, prints, the terrace through the glazing |
| Roof terrace | 0.882–0.944 | out through the glazing before the card arrives, then round to the sofa, loungers and fire bowl under the pergola with the sun going down |
| Summary | 0.96–1 | rise and pull back to the right; the lit rear elevation at blue hour |

Rules the route obeys, enforced by `node scripts/check-path.mjs` (exit 1 on a breach): lens at
least 0.3 m from any solid, authored look targets at least 2.4 m from the lens, pitch −35..+40°,
at most 12° of yaw and 6° of pitch per 0.005 of progress, lens shift within ±0.5 of the frame
height, consecutive card windows at least 0.016 apart, no uncaptioned stretch over 0.03, and
every rail landing inside its window. Orientation is interpolated as yaw and pitch, never as a
look point, so a target can never pass beside the lens and flip the view. Interiors stay level:
where a bed should sit low in the frame the key carries a vertical lens shift
(`camera.setViewOffset`) instead of a downward tilt, so verticals stay straight.

## Controls

- Scroll, trackpad or touch drives the flight; the rail on the right lists the chapters and a
  click lands on each chapter's best frame.
- Keyboard: `→` `↓` `PageDown` `Space` next chapter, `←` `↑` `PageUp` `Shift+Space` previous,
  `Home` / `End` the ends. The active chapter is announced to screen readers.
- Top right: **Day → Night** (the light follows the flight: daylight, sunset on the roof,
  night by the summary), **Day** or **Night** throughout; the **Interior** style pill shows
  from the facade to the roof terrace, where a change is visible, and the choice is kept in
  the URL hash (`#japandi`).
- The summary offers **Replay**, **Copy link to this style** and **Print the plans** (a plan
  sheet of the three floors drawn from `src/plan.js`, for print or save-as-PDF).
- `prefers-reduced-motion` removes the hover, bank and easing lag so the view follows the
  scroll directly. Without WebGL the loader stays as a page with the poster and the facts.

## Interior styles

Four presets researched from 2026 trend reporting (Dezeen, 1stDibs designer survey, Khaleej
Times on Dubai "restrained luxury", Bayut UAE trends, Domus Academy, paint colours of the
year): **Warm Minimal**, **Japandi Calm**, **Desert Organic**, **Aubergine Luxe**. A preset
re-parameterises shared material roles across the whole house (`src/styles.js`) and shows
its own living, dining and master-bedroom furniture (`src/stylesets.js`). Material types
never change, so switching recompiles nothing; a style's extra textures load on first use.
Every style keeps light walls and ceilings and sets a contrasting `joinery` role for doors,
wardrobes and kitchen runs (walnut, smoked black ash, caramel elm-oak, smoked walnut).
Deep link with `#japandi`, `#desert`, `#luxe` (or `?style=` for captures).

## Baked global illumination

`scripts/bake-gi.mjs` (Node + `three-mesh-bvh`, ~10 min at 256 rays) ray-traces an L1
spherical-harmonic irradiance volume at 0.5 m over the hero plot, using the page's own
geometry and finish albedos: sky light from the same Preetham model, sun light bounced off
surfaces, and each style's lamps as soft point emitters; two bounces each, probes inside
walls refilled from neighbours. The page mixes the parts for the moment (sky as the live env
map measures it, sun as it shines, lamps as dusk falls, the current style's lamps), and where
the volume says a point is enclosed its irradiance replaces the env map's diffuse light and
dims its reflections; outdoors the env map stays in charge. Interiors are then exposed about
two stops brighter by day, as an interiors photographer would, with lamps on dimmed. Re-run
the bake after moving walls, furniture or lamps:

```bash
npm install && npm run bake   # three-mesh-bvh is a dev dependency
```

## Volumetrics

`src/volumetric.js`: a half-resolution raymarch through the sun's static shadow map gives
shafts through glazing, pergola slats and palms, and a height-weighted haze; density is
per sample (dusty air inside the house volume, thin haze outside). Dust motes sparkle only
where the shadow map says sunlight reaches them; lamp cones fade in at dusk. The sun holds
south-west through the master suite, swings north-west for the terrace and sets at the
sunset view so the rays meet the visible sun.

## Performance decisions

- Sun direction is fixed; the shadow map renders once per zone (aerial 600 m, street 120 m,
  house 36 m) instead of every frame. Time of day changes light, sky and environment only.
- ~1 M triangles but ~150 draw calls: per-material merging for the hero, instancing for
  every neighbour, palm and shrub.
- Rendering sleeps when the scroll has settled; resolution adapts to frame time
  (pixel ratio 0.75–2). Quality tiers: `?q=high|mid|low` (mobile defaults to `low`: no AO,
  2048 shadows).
- Indoor light is two 3D texture fetches per pixel instead of area lights, re-mixed on the CPU
  only when the sky, sun, dusk or style actually changes.
- Debug/capture: `?p=0.42` renders a single still at that progress; `?tod=night` forces night;
  `&ui=0` hides the overlay; `&cam=x,y,z,lx,ly,lz[,fov[,shift]]` places the camera directly
  (with `?p=`) to compose a shot before writing it into `src/path.js`.
