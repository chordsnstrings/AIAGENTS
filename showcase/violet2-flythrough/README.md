# Violet 2 — TH12-4M townhouse flythrough (three.js)

Scroll-driven, real-time 3D flythrough of **plot XJ407B** (unit `VOT2/SD143/XJ407B`), a
four-bedroom **TH12-4M** mid-terrace townhouse in **Violet 2, DAMAC Hills 2, Dubai**.
The camera behaves like a stabilised drone: an aerial approach over the community, an
orbit to the street, then through the front door, every floor, out over the garden and
balconies, onto the roof terrace at sunset and back up into a blue-hour aerial.

```bash
npm install
npm run build     # bundles src/ -> dist/app.js (three.js r187 + n8ao)
npm run serve     # http://localhost:8080
```

Open `index.html` through any static server (module scripts do not run from `file://`).

## What is real and what is illustrative

Real, from the developer documents (sales offer + Violet brochure p.27):
plot 6.10 × 18.50 m (112.85 m²), room positions and sizes, three levels, two covered
parking spaces, saleable areas (589.22 / 632.92 / 296.12 / 834.00 sq ft = 2,352.26 sq ft),
facade language (white render frame, grey stone, bronze railings and lattice screen,
slatted timber pergolas), community drive times and amenities.

Illustrative: furniture and finishes, landscaping, the neighbouring cluster layout
(curving back-to-back rows modelled on the sales-offer location plan), lagoon and dunes.
The page says so in its summary card. Price and payment plan are deliberately not shown.

## Structure

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `src/plan.js` | Single source of truth: plan coordinates (x across plot, d from street, z = -d), levels, rooms. Drives both the 3D model and the SVG mini-plans. |
| `src/builder.js` | Accumulates boxes/quads/geometry per material key and emits one merged mesh (or one `InstancedMesh`) per material. Box UVs are in metres so textures tile at real-world scale. |
| `src/house.js` | Exterior shell of one townhouse. Hero copy has real balusters and open doors; neighbours get alpha railing cards and fake lit interiors. |
| `src/interior.js` | Hero-only interior: floors, linings, stacked dog-leg stair, doors, kitchen, bathrooms, furniture, interior lights. |
| `src/community.js` | Curving rows of 7-unit blocks around the hero (mirrored alternates), LOD blocks with a facade atlas further out, streets, lamps, palms, lagoon, desert. |
| `src/materials.js` | PBR sets from CC0 photo-scans (Poly Haven, ambientCG) packed as WebP colour / normal / AO-roughness; every finish named by the albedo it should read as; shader patches for tile joints, macro variation, texture contrast, fresnel glass, lamp-shade and opal-bulb glow, palm sway and the baked-GI lookup. |
| `src/gi.js`, `src/gi-data.js` | Baked irradiance volume (generated by `scripts/bake-gi.mjs`; do not edit the data file) mixed per moment into two 3D textures. |
| `src/softgoods.js` | Procedural duvets, pillows, throws and sheers: smooth noise plus a drape rule, no runtime simulation. |
| `src/atmosphere.js` | Preetham sky with lit clouds, sun + shadow zones, PMREM environment from the sky assigned to every lit material, two reflection probes for the glazing, fog, stars, time of day. |
| `src/path.js` | Keyframed drone route (centripetal Catmull-Rom + monotone progress mapping) and gimbal rig (lag, bank, hover). |
| `src/post.js` | N8AO ambient occlusion, volumetric light, bloom, ACES output, grade/vignette/grain, SMAA. |
| `src/volumetric.js` | Shadow-map raymarched light shafts and haze, dust motes, lamp cones. |
| `src/styles.js`, `src/stylesets.js` | Interior style presets (materials, named by the albedo a finish should read as) and their furniture sets. |
| `src/ui.js` | Loader, chapter cards, rail, mini-plans, plot pin. |

## Interior styles

Four presets researched from 2026 trend reporting (Dezeen, 1stDibs designer survey, Khaleej
Times on Dubai "restrained luxury", Bayut UAE trends, Domus Academy, paint colours of the
year): **Warm Minimal**, **Japandi Calm**, **Desert Organic**, **Aubergine Luxe**. A preset
re-parameterises shared material roles across the whole house (`src/styles.js`) and shows
its own living, dining and master-bedroom furniture (`src/stylesets.js`). Material types
never change, so switching recompiles nothing; a style's extra textures load on first use.
Every style keeps light walls and ceilings and sets a contrasting `joinery` role for doors,
wardrobes and kitchen runs (walnut, smoked black ash, caramel elm-oak, smoked walnut).
Deep link with `#japandi`, `#desert`, `#luxe` (or `?style=` for captures).

Every textured role names its finish by **albedo**, the sRGB colour it should read as: the styler
divides the scan's measured mean (`SET_AVG`) out of it, so a white duvet renders white rather than
the scan's grey times a tint, and the GI bake lights the rooms with the same numbers. A role's
`rough` is likewise the finish's real mean roughness: three multiplies the material roughness by
the packed roughness map, whose mean (`SET_ROUGH`) is divided out, so honed stone no longer turns
into a mirror of the sky. Velvet roles add `flat`, which pulls the crushed scan towards the colour
so the sheen, not the texture noise, reads as velvet. A style switch resolves every texture first
and the last click wins, so a failed download leaves nothing half-applied.

## Lighting and the look

Golden hour to blue hour is three keyframes in `src/atmosphere.js` (a dust-hazed Gulf sky:
turbidity 8, little Rayleigh, a wide Mie glow, a few high wisps); the sun holds still per zone so
the shadow map renders once. The environment map is assigned to every lit material explicitly,
so per-material and per-style `envMapIntensity` values count; environments are cached as render
targets (LRU, the bake's reference pinned) and the asynchronous sky read-back behind the baked
light is validated before it is trusted. Street-front and garden-facing glazing mirror two local
reflection probes (256 px cube renders, refreshed on load and at steps of the evening), so windows
show the street and the garden instead of reading as holes. Lamp shades are linen over the bulb:
the shade shader grades the glow from the rim to the top and burns hotter on the inside, opal
globes darken towards their rim, and every bulb position is recorded (`interior.lamps`, each
style kit's `lamps`) for a pool of night point lights. The photographic numbers (indoor exposure,
fill, lamp level, look-in weight, bloom ramp) live in `src/look.js`.

## Baked global illumination

`scripts/bake-gi.mjs` (Node + `three-mesh-bvh`, ~10 min at 256 rays) ray-traces an L1
spherical-harmonic irradiance volume at 0.5 m over the hero plot, using the page's own
geometry and finish albedos: sky light from the same Preetham model, sun light bounced off
surfaces, and each style's lamps as soft point emitters; two bounces each, probes inside
walls refilled from neighbours. The page mixes the parts for the moment (sky as the live env
map measures it, sun as it shines, lamps as dusk falls, the current style's lamps), and where
the volume says a point is enclosed its irradiance replaces the env map's diffuse light, and the
env reflections are dimmed by the probe's open-sky fraction and tinted by the room's own bounce
colour; outdoors the env map stays in charge. Interiors are then exposed about
two stops brighter by day, as an interiors photographer would, with lamps on dimmed. Re-run
the bake after moving walls, furniture or lamps:

```bash
npm install && npm run bake   # three-mesh-bvh is a dev dependency
```

## Volumetrics

`src/volumetric.js`: a half-resolution raymarch through the sun's static shadow map gives
shafts through glazing, pergola slats and palms, and a height-weighted haze; density is
per sample (dusty air inside the house volume, thin haze outside). Dust motes sparkle only
where the shadow map says sunlight reaches them; lamp cones fade in at dusk. The sun holds
south-west through the master suite, swings north-west for the terrace and sets at the
sunset view so the rays meet the visible sun.

## Performance decisions

- Three quality tiers are pixel budgets, not device-pixel-ratio caps (`src/tiers.js`): `high`
  3.7 MP, `mid` 2.1 MP, `low` 0.6 MP of device pixels per frame. The tier comes from the GPU name
  (`WEBGL_debug_renderer_info`): discrete GPUs and Apple Pro/Max chips `high`, integrated laptop
  GPUs, base M1/M2 and iPads `mid`, phones and software renderers `low`; `?q=high|mid|low`
  overrides it. The pixel ratio follows from the budget and the canvas box, and an adaptive
  scaler walks rungs of 0.6–1.3× that ratio on measured frame cost (the GPU timer where the
  browser offers one), stepping up only once the scroll has settled.
- Sun direction is fixed; the shadow map (2048² on every tier) renders once per zone (aerial
  600 m, street 120 m, house 36 m) and during the terrace sun swing. Far houses and far palms
  cast only from the air, so the swing redraws about 0.4 M caster triangles instead of 1.1 M.
- Palms are two instanced models, 1,112 and 192 triangles, split at 70 m from the camera and
  re-partitioned once it has moved 5 m. A frame is 0.77–0.81 M triangles (was 1.21–1.27 M) in
  142–179 draw calls: per-material merging for the hero, instancing for every neighbour, palm
  and shrub, opaque community meshes drawn after the hero so interiors are not shaded twice.
- The post chain is two full-resolution passes: the scene through N8AO (a plain render pass on
  `low` outside the house and street zones) and one finish pass that adds the half-resolution
  volumetric march and the bloom in linear light, then tone-maps, encodes and grades. SMAA runs
  only while the pixel ratio is under 1.75; `low` skips the volumetric march.
- Textures: 18 base sets; the 11 exterior ones are awaited, the 7 interior-only sets stream in
  behind the first frame (the camera starts in the air). Phones read `assets/tex/512/`
  (3.0 MB for all 29 sets against 8.6 MB), about 58 MiB of texture memory instead of 232 MiB.
- Rendering sleeps when the scroll has settled. Indoor light is two 3D texture fetches per
  pixel instead of area lights, re-mixed on the CPU with weights quantised to 1/128, at most
  every third frame while the light eases.
- The drawing buffer, camera and overlay are sized from the canvas box (the large viewport)
  through a `ResizeObserver` with a 150 ms debounce, so a phone's toolbar never reallocates the
  render targets mid-scroll. A lost WebGL context shows a notice and reloads on restore.
- Debug/capture: `?p=0.42` renders a single still at that progress (every texture set is
  awaited, so stills are complete); `?tod=night` forces night; `&ui=0` hides the overlay.
  Regenerate the 512 tier after re-packing sets: Lanczos to 512 px, WebP quality 82, method 6,
  files already at 512 px copied.
